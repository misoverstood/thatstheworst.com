I was searching for a personal knowledge base or wiki/faq for a friend and came across these links. Any other suggestions?  
  
create your own wiki: <http://twiki.org/>  
  
simple kb: <http://68kb.com/>  
  
wiki software based on asp.net: <http://www.screwturn.eu/>  
  
php based FAQ software: <http://www.phpmyfaq.de/>  
  
open source enterprise documentation: <http://www.mindtouch.com/>  
  
another open source wiki: <http://moinmo.in/>  
  
knowledge management tool: <http://bestpractical.com/rtfm/>  
  
another simple and easy option would be to create a blog (using tumblr.com or wordpress.com or wordpress.org) or a forum (using phpbb.com or similar)  
  
wordpress has tonnes of plugins that can extend the functionality of your blog so for instance: cms, wikis, help desk tickets, etc  
  
you can find most of them here: <http://wordpress.org/extend/plugins/>
